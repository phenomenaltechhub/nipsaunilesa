## Welcome to NIPSA UNILESA

**0:09 · navigation**

**0:20 · resources**

## Student Portal and study tools

**0:56 · portal**

The Student Portal brings together public academic support and links. Pharmaideia is introduced as an upcoming separate application; registration is not open.

**1:08 · tools**

Open Study Tools for GPA estimates, a weekly timetable and revision planning. These tools store entries only in this browser on this device.

**1:18 · grading-scale**

For GPA, review the sample grading scale first. It is editable and is not verified University grading policy.

**1:28 · gpa-example**

Here is an illustrative calculation, not a student's record. Three units at five points and two units at four points give an estimated GPA of four point six zero.

**1:45 · planning-tools**

Use Timetable for your own schedule, and the study planner to prioritise topics. Export a backup before clearing browser storage.
