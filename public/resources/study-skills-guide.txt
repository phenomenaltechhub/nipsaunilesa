NIPSA UNILESA STUDY SKILLS GUIDE

Distributed by NIPSA UNILESA Chapter - Department of Pharmacology, University of Ilesa.

This document is a public student support material prepared by NIPSAUNILESA.

Purpose
This guide supports study planning, revision, and healthy academic routines in the Department of Pharmacology.

1. Plan weekly study blocks
- Set realistic timetable blocks for lectures, revision, practical preparation, and rest.
- Keep one short review session after each class.

2. Focus on understanding first
- Convert difficult ideas into your own words.
- Connect class notes to lab work and difficult concepts.

3. Build reliable revision habits
- Use summary sheets, diagrams, and recall prompts.
- Review older topics regularly before assessments.

4. Use active learning
- Practice with short answer questions and case studies.
- Explain a concept to a classmate or study group.

5. Protect academic wellbeing
- Schedule rest, sleep, and exercise.
- Seek guidance early if workload or motivation feels overwhelming.
