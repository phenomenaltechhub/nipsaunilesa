NIPSA UNILESA ORIENTATION CHECKLIST

Distributed by NIPSAUNILESA — Department of Pharmacology, University of Ilesa.

This checklist is a public student support resource prepared by NIPSAUNILESA.

Before lectures begin
- Confirm your student registration details and department contacts.
- Check the academic calendar for lectures, practicals, and assessment deadlines.
- Review the course structure and identify required reading materials.

During the first month
- Attend orientation and department notices.
- Introduce yourself to classmates and study group leaders.
- Identify the key people for academic advice and general support.

Study habits
- Keep notes in a consistent format.
- Review lab instructions before practical sessions.
- Meet with peers to compare learning materials and clarify concepts.

Student wellbeing
- Keep track of your workload.
- Maintain healthy routines and avoid last-minute cramming.
- Contact the department or student support team if you need guidance.
